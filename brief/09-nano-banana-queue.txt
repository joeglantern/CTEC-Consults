THE FORMULA (why it works)
Realism comes from camera and light language, not from grime. Every prompt says: real photograph not a render,
shot on 35mm film, available light only, natural grain, shallow focus, slightly off-centre framing.
Every prompt also says the surfaces are CLEAN and WELL KEPT and the materials are good (walnut, brushed steel,
matte black, polished concrete, linen). Never say worn, scuffed, scratched, dusty, cluttered, stained, chipped:
that reads poor, not premium.

PEOPLE RULE: no faces anywhere. People only appear as a cropped detail when the shot needs one, and those are
dark-skinned Kenyan hands, cropped at the wrists, well groomed.
