# About page: the six values cards

Six cards in the deck on the About page, one for each core value in the profile. These cards are dark,
stacked over the network clip, so unlike the home page cards these images follow the dark register of
the rest of the site: one subject, one warm light, everything else falling to black. Portrait, because
the cards are taller than they are wide.

Run them in ChatGPT one at a time. Save each result as the filename shown into `site/public/img/real/`
and tell me. I wire them into the cards.

Rules in every prompt: real photograph, 35mm film, available light, 4:5 portrait, no people, no hands,
no faces, no text, no logos, no watermark.

---
