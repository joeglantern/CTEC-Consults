# "What sets us apart" card images

Five cards, one image each. The profile lists five differentiators, so there are five, not six. The cards
sit on parchment, so unlike the rest of the site these images are bright: pale surfaces, warm daylight,
bone and parchment tones. Each one is a single object or place that says the line on its card without
illustrating it literally, shot with strong perspective so the eye travels into the frame.

Run them in ChatGPT one at a time. Save each result as the filename shown into `site/public/img/real/`
and tell me. I wire them into the cards.

Rules that apply to every prompt (already written into each one): real photograph, 35mm film, natural
daylight, no people, no hands, no faces, no text, no logos, no watermark, 3:2 landscape.

---

### 1. `why-technology.jpg`
Card: Technology-backed. "Every recommendation is grounded in technology that drives efficiency and scale."

```
A real photograph, not a render, 3:2 landscape. Shot on 35mm film in natural daylight. Subject: a low
camera angle looking straight down a long run of neatly bundled pale fibre optic cables inside a clean,
bright equipment room, the bundle converging to a single vanishing point far ahead, pale bone coloured
walls and floor, soft warm daylight from the side. Strong one point perspective, shallow depth of field
so only the near cables are sharp. Natural film grain. Everything immaculate and orderly. No people, no
hands, no text, no logos, no watermark.
```

### 2. `why-reach.jpg`

```

photographed from a low angle across its surface so the ridges and valleys catch warm raking sunlight
from a window and throw long soft shadows, the far edge of the continent falling out of focus. Strong

Recommended: a solution designed for one place.

```
